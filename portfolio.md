## en

A tool in development for reviewing contacts by case and distinguishing your next action from a reply awaited from someone else. The design covers CLI, MCP and dashboard access to a contact ledger. The public main branch primarily contains design material, while implementation proceeds on a development branch. The overview video presents work in progress rather than promising generally released features.
